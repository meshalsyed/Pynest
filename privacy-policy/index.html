Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This page is a general template provided for transparency and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Please have it reviewed by qualified legal counsel before relying on it for compliance purposes.

What We Collect

When you enquire via our website, WhatsApp, or email, we collect the information you provide directly — typically your name, email address, phone number, and any stay preferences or messages you share.

We also use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website (pages viewed, general location, device type). This data is anonymized where possible and is not linked back to you individually.

How We Use It

  • To respond to enquiries and confirm bookings
  • To coordinate check-in, concierge services, and stay logistics
  • To send booking-related updates via WhatsApp, email, or phone
  • To improve our website and services

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

WhatsApp Communication

Much of our guest communication happens over WhatsApp. By messaging us or submitting an enquiry form that opens WhatsApp, you consent to being contacted there regarding your enquiry or booking.

Third-Party Services

We use third-party tools including Google Analytics and Google Maps. These providers may collect data according to their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

Data Retention

We retain enquiry and booking information only as long as necessary to provide our services and meet legal or accounting obligations.

Your Rights
