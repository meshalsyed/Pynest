Paradise Beach

A stretch of clean, quiet sand reachable only by a short boat ride through the Chunnambar backwaters — one of the more unusual half-day trips near Pondicherry.

Paradise Beach is separated from the mainland by the Chunnambar backwaters, so the only way in is by boat — a short ride that's part of the appeal. Boats depart from the Chunnambar Boat House, a short drive south of central Pondicherry.

Because access is limited to boat traffic, the beach stays noticeably cleaner and calmer than more accessible stretches of coastline. It's a popular half-day trip, particularly for families and anyone wanting a swim away from the crowds.

Plan for a few hours — the boat ride, the beach itself, and time back at the boat house — and go earlier in the day to avoid the midday heat and any queues for the boat.
